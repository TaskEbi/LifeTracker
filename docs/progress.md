## M13 · Boss raids, focus mode, look-back (done, needs your review)
- Weekly boss (`src/domain/progress/boss.ts`): each week (from 26 Oct 2026) one of eight yōkai bosses, drawn by date in rounds of eight weeks (each boss once a round, never twice running), nothing stored. Everything logged that week hits it: 1 damage per minute (its weak section ×2), 25 for a section's first time that week, 10 per task, 10 per night (+10 bed on time), 5 per screen-time day (+15 under target). HP is your last four weeks' average +10% (300–1500), so bosses grow with you. Defeating it is a keyed XP event (`boss:<monday>`, 100 XP, under Quests). Drawn masks (`BossArt.tsx`) that shake when hit and get a 討伐 stamp when beaten.

  - Four achievements: First Hunt, Oni Hunter (5), Yōkai Bane (20), Full Bestiary (all eight): 45 in all.
- Focus mode (`src/features/focus`): a timer for violin, coding, study, chess or sports with an optional goal (15–90 min or open). Kept on the device (survives reloads, keeps counting when the phone is locked), shown as a pill above the command bar on every page, buzzes once at the goal where the phone allows it. Finishing opens that section's own log sheet with the minutes filled in; nothing is saved until you save there, and the waiting minutes clear once a session in that section is saved. Discard asks first. Start it from Home, or "start focus timer" in the command palette.
