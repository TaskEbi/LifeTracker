# Working rules

LifeTracker is a personal tracker (React 19 + Vite + TypeScript PWA) that shares Curio Cabinet's Supabase database. Read `docs/overview.md` first, then `docs/progress.md`.

## Data safety
- Never write to the production database from development. Sync is off unless `VITE_LT_ALLOW_SYNC=true` **and** the Supabase variables are set; only Production builds get them.
- Schema changes are additive migrations in `supabase/migrations/` (new `lt_` tables or nullable columns). Never drop, rename or tighten anything Curio Cabinet uses.
- Migration ownership: from `20261025120000_lifetracker_core.sql` on, **this repository owns the schema**. The AnyStop repository must not add migrations while both apps run. The first nine files are copies of AnyStop's, byte for byte, so `supabase db push` sees them as already applied.
- Production migration, replacing Curio Cabinet and deploying are separate release steps that need the user's review. Follow `docs/migration.md`.
- Keep secrets out of the repo and the bundle: only the publishable (anon) key may appear in `VITE_` variables.

## One record per activity
- Time spent → `log_entries` (the Daily Log row). Section detail goes in `lt_details` with the **same id**.
- Sleep / screen time → `log_metrics` (one row per metric per day, id from `metricId`). Bed/wake → `lt_nights`.
- Gym → the Daily Log entry plus `lt_details` with the same id; the detail lists the `muscles` hit (no templates, no plan, no sets). Muscle statistics are derived (`src/features/gym/model.ts`), never stored.
- Tasks → `todos` (+ `section`). Cabinet → `items` / `rooms` / `connections`. Bucket list → `lt_bucket` (done when `done_at` is set).
- EPQ → time is Study sessions whose subject is the project's (default `EPQ`); the project, milestones, production log, sources and daily word counts are `lt_records` kinds `epq_*` under the study section (`src/features/epq/actions.ts`). No migration.
- Life pages (`src/features/{mood,money,people,media,places}`) → `lt_records` under the cabinet section: mood is the Hideout's `hq_checkin` row (weather, energy, stress, tags); money `mn_tx` and `mn_goal` (wallet and goal totals are derived, never stored); people `pp_person`, `pp_gift`, `pp_time`; media `md_item`; places `pl_place`, `pl_trip`, `pl_out`. No migration, no XP.
- Stage (`src/features/stage`, logic in `src/domain/stage`) → no table of its own: a test is a Study mark (`study_mark`) with extra fields; a match or race is a sports session whose `lt_details` gains opponent, sets, stats or race name and time; violin performances, chess games, talks and anything else are `lt_records` kind `pf_show` under the cabinet section; getting ready for a calendar day is `pf_prep` (id from the occurrence key). A day planned ahead is a calendar event with `data.stage` and `data.plan` (kind, where, opp, comp, sport, km, subject; pieces in `data.pieces`); logging it later reads the plan to fill the form in (`savePlan` in `src/features/stage/store.ts`). Sheets that edit those rows must keep fields they don't show. No migration, no XP of its own.
- Violin repertoire → the same `piece` records as the stand (past pieces archived), with optional `started`, `finished` (YYYY or YYYY-MM), `level`, `note`, `fav`.
- Past papers (`src/features/papers`, logic and data in `src/domain/papers`) → `lt_records` under the study section: `ppq_paper` (one per paper, id from `pairId('ppq:paper', '<unit>|<session>')`: done date, score, minutes, kept for a mock), `ppq_hard` (one per hard question: done, or wrong with a retry due), `ppq_topic` (a topic booklet ticked), `ppq_mistake` (a mistake to retry). A score can also write one `study_mark` (its id kept as `markId`), and the time is an ordinary Study session. The paper list is generated by `scripts/papers-catalogue.mjs` into `src/domain/papers/catalogue.ts` (don't edit it by hand) and loads in its own chunk. No migration, no XP of its own.
- Study notes (`src/features/notes`, content and logic in `src/domain/notes`) → the notes themselves are static files (`src/domain/notes/content/<subject>.ts`, written in the small markup of `markup.ts`); what you've ticked as understood is one `lt_records` row per topic under the study section, kind `nt_read` (id from `pairId('nt:read', '<topic id>')`, `secs` = the section ids ticked; no row when nothing is ticked). A2 units carry `a2: true` and follow the AS units. The study tools (flash cards, formula sheet, glossary, tips and mistakes; `src/domain/notes/tools.ts`, `src/features/notes/Tools.tsx`) are derived from the notes' content, never written twice; only how well each flash card is known is kept, one row per topic, kind `nt_card` (id from `pairId('nt:card', '<topic id>')`, `cards` = `{ <card key>: [box 1–5, due day] }`). Which level is shown is a choice of the device (localStorage). The look is the CS revision site's, scoped to `.nt` with classes prefixed `nt-`. Loads in its own chunk. No migration, no XP of its own.
- Maths → `lt_records` kinds `sitting` (a paper, real or practice) and `problem`; a real contest with a date also has an `lt_events` row tagged `data.maths`.
- Hideout (review, cards, check-ins, niggles, gear, predictions, capsules, rituals) → `lt_records` with `hq_*` kinds under the cabinet section (`src/features/hq/store.ts`). A row holds 32 KB at most, so long text needs a limit.
- Album (a face and memories per day) → one `lt_records` row per photo, kind `album_shot`, under the cabinet section (`src/features/album/store.ts`). The image is a blob on the device until sync uploads it to Curio Cabinet's private `photos` bucket (`src/lib/photos.ts`); the row keeps the path and a small inline thumbnail. A memory can be a video (`media: 'video'`, `dur`, at most 60 s and 50 MB, kept as it is): it uploads to LifeTracker's own private `lt-videos` bucket (`album-video/…`), since `photos` takes images only.
- Hangar spending (purchases, chests, capsules, claims, loadout) → `lt_records` kind `gm_tx` under the cabinet section (`src/features/game/store.ts`). Currencies earned are **derived** from the records (`src/domain/game/earn.ts`); never store a balance.
- Motion (`src/features/motion`, numbers in `src/domain/motion`) → nothing stored: nine 3D scenes at `/motion` drawn from levels and the Daily Log; sample numbers (labelled on screen) when fewer than five days are logged. One file per scene in `scenes/`, behind the contract in `stage.ts`; three.js loads only with this page. No migration, no XP.
- The Works (the clicker in the Hangar, `/hangar/works`; logic in `src/domain/game/idle.ts`, saving in `src/features/game/worksStore.ts`) → one `lt_records` row, kind `gm_idle` (id `pairId('gm', 'idle')`), under the cabinet section: counts, lifetime totals and the time bolts were last settled. Bolts made while away are worked out from that time when it's opened. Gold cogs are never a stored balance: the save counts cogs ever earned, and each thing bought at the counter is a `gm_tx` row of type `works` (id `pairId('gm:works', '<item>')`), whose price comes from `WORKS_GOODS`. Robot cosmetics sold there carry `works: true` in `catalog.ts` and are sold nowhere else. The factory's own look is the pref `game_works_look`. No migration, no XP, and it never pays Ryō or Tama.
- The Aura Dial (a room in the Works, `/hangar/works/aura`; logic in `src/domain/game/aura.ts`, saving in `src/features/game/auraStore.ts`, drawing in `auraFx.ts` and `auraFxMore.ts`, each aura's look and scene wind-up as data in `auraLooks.ts`) → one `lt_records` row, kind `gm_aura` (id `pairId('gm', 'aura')`), under the cabinet section: motes, roll counts, times each aura was rolled, upgrade levels, the aura worn, and `queue` (scenes of rare auras not watched yet). It rolls live only while its room is open; other time is rolled from the row's `at` when the app or the room is next opened (`AuraGate` in the shell loads the watcher only when the row exists). Gold cogs the index pays and its bonus to the factory are **derived** from the auras found (`auraCogs`, `worksBonus`), never stored. An aura not yet rolled must not be drawn anywhere. No migration, no XP, and it never pays Ryō or Tama.
- XP, levels, streaks and achievements are **derived** (`src/domain/progress`). Never store XP.

## Code
- Feature modules live in `src/features/<name>/` and own their screens, sheets and actions. Shared pieces: `src/data` (records, sync), `src/domain` (pure logic, tested), `src/ui/i` (instrument components), `src/app` (shell, router).
- Curio Cabinet code under `src/features/cabinet`, `src/ui` and `src/data` keeps its own conventions; LifeTracker CSS classes are prefixed `i-` and tokens avoid Cabinet's names.
- Label sample or simulated data on screen. Never show a save or connection as successful unless it was.
- `npm test` (domain + PGlite database tests) and `npm run typecheck` must pass before committing.
